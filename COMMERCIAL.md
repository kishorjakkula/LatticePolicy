# Commercial Support And Services

LatticePolicy is free, open-source software licensed under the Apache License
2.0. Nothing in this document changes that. The community edition is not a
trial, a limited tier, or a teaser for a closed-source product: it is the
same codebase that carriers, integrators, and individual contributors can
run, fork, modify, and deploy today, with no license fees and no feature
gating.

This document explains what support the community can expect for free, and
what kinds of paid help may be available for organizations that want it.

## What Is Free

- The full LatticePolicy source code, under the Apache License 2.0.
- Public documentation under `docs/`, including architecture, API, domain
  model, deployment, and AI-contributor guides.
- Community support through GitHub:
  - [Issues](https://github.com/kishorjakkula/LatticePolicy/issues) for bug
    reports and feature requests.
  - [Discussions](https://github.com/kishorjakkula/LatticePolicy/discussions)
    (if enabled on the repository) for questions and design conversations.
- Review of contributions submitted under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md),
  on a best-effort basis by maintainers.

Community support has no response-time guarantee. Maintainers and
contributors help as availability allows.

## What Paid Services May Be Available

Some organizations need help beyond what community support can reasonably
provide: dedicated response times, hands-on implementation work, or
production operational support. The project maintainer(s) may be available,
on a paid basis, for work such as:

- **Implementation support** — helping a team stand up LatticePolicy for a
  specific carrier, agency, or MGA, including product pack and tenant
  configuration.
- **Managed hosting** — operating a LatticePolicy deployment on behalf of an
  organization that does not want to run its own infrastructure.
- **Enterprise deployment help** — production architecture review, scaling,
  CI/CD, and deployment hardening beyond the examples in
  [docs/CLOUD_DEPLOYMENT.md](docs/CLOUD_DEPLOYMENT.md) and
  [docs/GITHUB_ACTIONS_AWS.md](docs/GITHUB_ACTIONS_AWS.md).
- **Compliance and security assistance** — help adapting tenant isolation,
  RBAC, audit, and data-handling behavior to a specific carrier's regulatory
  or security review requirements.
- **Product configuration services** — building or adapting product packs,
  rating tables, forms, and underwriting rules for a specific line of
  business or state footprint.

These are illustrative categories, not a fixed price list or a commitment
that every category is staffed at all times. No pricing is published here;
pricing and availability are discussed directly with prospective customers.

## What This Is Not

- Paid services do not unlock hidden features. Everything in this
  repository is available to every user under the same open-source license.
- Purchasing support does not change your rights or obligations under the
  Apache License 2.0.
- This document does not create a support contract by itself. Any paid
  engagement is a separate agreement negotiated directly with the requesting
  organization.

## How To Get In Touch

- For bugs, feature requests, and general community support, use
  [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/kishorjakkula/LatticePolicy/issues).
- For security vulnerabilities, follow the private reporting process in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of opening a public issue.
- For commercial support, managed hosting, or implementation-services
  inquiries, open a
  [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/kishorjakkula/LatticePolicy/discussions)
  or a GitHub Issue labeled as a commercial inquiry, and a maintainer will
  follow up. This keeps a single, auditable contact path instead of relying
  on private channels.
